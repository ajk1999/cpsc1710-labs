What you can get today, from your own AI

Several requests are things your assistant can do right now, adjusted to your background:

  • “Explain this file one block at a time. I am new to Python. Before each block, ask me to guess what it does.”
  • “Make a one-page cheat sheet of the Python in this file: each function, what goes in, what comes out.”
  • “I work in finance. Give me two places this idea matters in my field.”
